## 5. What the Phase 3 numbers say

This section is my reading of the tables above. It is a judgment, not a further test.

**The gate.** Twelve cells pass the gate as you defined it. Two pairs among them are the same test, run on two bar sizes: V2 USDJPY on M15 and H1 (100% the same trades), and V2 USTEC on M15 and H1 (98.9%). That leaves **10 independent survivors**.

**The evidence that any of them is an edge rather than luck is weak:**

1. **No survivor clears the multiple-testing bar.** With 164 cells tested, Bonferroni needs t(R) ≥ 3.61. The best survivor has 2.46.
2. **Fewer cells look good than chance alone would produce.** Only 4 cells reach t ≥ 1.645, where independent cells with no edge would give about 8. Two of the 4 are the V2 USTEC near-duplicate pair. After costs, the whole grid looks like what a market with no edge for these rules would produce.
3. **Most survivors lost money in-sample.** Nothing was tuned on 2023, so 2023 is a second independent test. Six of the 10 independent survivors had in-sample PF below 1: M2 H1 USTEC, S1 XAUUSD, T2 H1 USTEC, T3 H1 USTEC, V2 USDJPY and V2 USTEC. Only three survivors had PF above 1.15 in both years: V1 H1 USDJPY, T1 M15 USTEC and T1 M15 XAUUSD.
4. **The USTEC trend survivors mostly rode one rising year.** USTEC rose 24.6% in 2024. Long trades produced:
   - M2 H1: $20,194 of $22,653;
   - T1 M15: $13,884 of $14,374;
   - T2 H1: $19,341 of $16,603 (its short trades lost money).
5. **Swap could remove most of the provisional survivors.** The USTEC trend cells fail the gate if financing exceeds 4.1–8.7% a year on notional. Index CFD long financing is usually a benchmark rate plus a broker markup. The US benchmark rate was 5.25–5.50% for most of 2024 (Fed target range), so these cells could go either way. T1 M15 XAUUSD fails above 0.85% a year. The exact verdicts need your broker's numbers.
6. **The two most consistent cells** survive all three runs and do not depend on long-only drift:
   - **V1 H1 USDJPY** (squeeze breakout):
     - PF 1.42 in 2023 and 1.58 in 2024;
     - long and short P/L about equal;
     - swap break-even 16% a year, far above the 2024 USD–JPY rate gap (Fed at most 5.50%, BoJ at most 0.25%);
     - but t = 1.65, and it has 125 trades.
   - **V2 (volatility breakout, same-day, no swap)**: pooled PF across the 4 symbols is 1.24, but it lost money in 2023 on both symbols where it survives.

**Overall:** the gate is passed, but on one out-of-sample year of data the results do not show an edge that stands out from chance. Combining these survivors in Phase 4 can still be done, and Phase 4's deflated Sharpe and the holdout are built to catch a combination of noise. A longer history would be the stronger test.
